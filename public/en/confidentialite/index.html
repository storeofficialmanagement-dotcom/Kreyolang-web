This policy explains which personal data Kreyolang uses, why, for how long, who it is shared with, and how to exercise your rights. It applies the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU Regulation 2016/679), French law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 (“Informatique et Libertés”) and Article L34-5 of the French Postal and Electronic Communications Code. The French version is the reference version.

Article 2 — The data we use

2.1 Your account: first name, last name, username and handle (@), email address and date of birth. Your password is encrypted by our authentication provider: we never have access to it. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (possibly hidden by Apple) and an identifier from that service.

2.2 Your learning: languages learned, lessons and sections completed, answers to exercises, experience points (XP), gems, lives, day streaks, league and ranking, badges, daily goal, practice time for the day and the time chosen for the training reminder, reason for learning.

2.3 Your interactions with other players: follows and friend requests, private messages, challenges and duels, likes, reports you make and people you block.

2.4 Your settings: interface language, time zone, avatar appearance, your notification choices, your choice about usage statistics and your choice about news and offers (with the date and the version of the text you accepted).

2.5 Technical data: your account's unique identifier, the notification token of each of your devices, system (iOS or Android) and app version, error reports, notification delivery tracking (notification sent, received by Apple or Google, or rejected). If you accepted usage statistics: the actions taken in the app (screens opened, lessons started), linked to your identifier and your email address.

2.6 Payment: your bank details are handled only by Apple or Google. Through RevenueCat, we only receive the status of your subscription (active or not, plan, renewal date).

2.7 What we do not collect: no location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising identifier. Kreyolang shows no third-party advertising and sells no data.

2.8 Mandatory data: an email address (or Apple/Google sign-in), a username and a date of birth are required to create an account — the date of birth is used to check that you are at least 15. Everything else is optional or depends on how you use the app.

Article 3 — What other players see

Your username and handle, your avatar, the languages you learn, your XP and league rank, your badges and, where applicable, your Premium status. Your first name, last name, date of birth and email address are never shown to other players. Your private messages are visible only to you and the person you are writing to.

Article 5 — News and offers

We send you promotional messages (app news, subscription offers) only if you have agreed, by ticking the box “Get our news and offers by notification and email” or by turning this choice on in Settings. This box is never pre-ticked, and refusing changes nothing about how you use Kreyolang.

Messages tailored to your journey. To send you a useful rather than generic message, we may take your progress into account: free sections completed, current day streak, language learned, time since your last visit. If you are under 18, you only receive general messages, never tailored to your journey.

Change your mind at any time, as easily as you agreed: Settings → Notifications → “News and offers”, with one switch for notifications and one for emails. Every promotional email also contains a free unsubscribe link. Your withdrawal takes effect immediately for later messages.

Duration: your consent is used until you withdraw it, and at most 3 years after your last activity in the app. After that, we send you nothing without asking for your consent again.

Consent given in the app concerns Kreyolang only: your data is never passed on to partners for their own marketing.

Article 8 — Your rights

You have the following rights:

  • Access (Art. 15): get a copy of your data. From the app, export your data in JSON format, or by email.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): from your profile, or by email.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): Settings → My account → Delete my account, or on kreyolang.com (“Delete my account”), or by email. Deletion is permanent: progress, XP, gems and streaks are lost.
  • Portability (Art. 20): receive your data in a structured format (JSON).
  • Withdrawing your consent (Art. 7(3)), at any time, without affecting what was done before: notifications (Settings → Notifications), news and offers (Settings → Notifications → News and offers, or the link in each email), usage statistics (Settings → Experience).
  • Objection (Art. 21): you can object at any time, without giving a reason, to promotional messages and the related profiling. You can also object to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation.
  • Restriction (Art. 18): ask for a processing operation to be temporarily frozen, for example while a disputed piece of data is checked.
  • Instructions after your death (French Data Protection Act, Art. 85): say what should happen to your data after your death.

How to do it: from the app where indicated, otherwise by writing to contact@kreyolang.com from your account's email address (otherwise we may ask you to prove your identity). We reply within one month, which may be extended by two months for a complex request; we will then let you know within the first month.

Complaint: if you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the CNIL (French data protection authority) — 3, place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France — www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes.

Article 9 — Minimum age

You must be at least 15 to create a Kreyolang account; your date of birth is used to check this. From 15, you can consent on your own to the use of your data (French Data Protection Act, Art. 45). Kreyolang collects no sensitive data (health, opinions, precise location, biometrics). A parent who finds that a child under 15 has created an account can write to us to have it deleted.

Article 10 — Security

Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted account by account: your first name, last name, date of birth, email address, choices about offers and private messages are accessible only to you (and to the other person for a message). Passwords are encrypted by our authentication provider. Sensitive operations (gems, XP, subscription) are validated by our servers, not by the device. In the event of a data breach presenting a risk to you, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours and, if the risk is high, we inform you directly (GDPR Art. 33 and 34).

Article 11 — Cookies and trackers

In the app: only what is needed for the app to work is used without your consent (keeping you signed in, preferences). Usage statistics (PostHog) only run after your consent, given or refused at first launch and changeable in Settings → Experience. No advertising tracker is used.

On kreyolang.com: the site sets no cookies and uses no audience measurement tool. It only keeps your language choice in your browser's storage. To offer you the right language straight away, it queries the ipapi.co service, which receives your device's IP address and returns the matching country; nothing is recorded by Kreyolang.

Article 12 — Changes to this policy

We may change this policy to follow changes in the app or the law. The version in force is always published on this page, with its date. In the event of a significant change to your rights or to how we use your data, we let you know by email or in the app at least 30 days before it takes effect. A new use based on consent (for example news and offers) only applies to you if you accept it.
